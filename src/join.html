Application journey

A clear, structured way to join student clubs.

Follow the steps below to discover clubs, share your interests, and meet leadership teams. The process is consistent across categories so you always know what to expect at each stage.

Step 01

Explore clubs and shortlist your top choices

Visit the clubs overview page, filter by category, and identify two to four clubs that align with your goals, schedule, and energy levels. Look for clubs that complement your academic path rather than mirror it.

20–30 minutes Start from the Clubs page

Step 02

Submit a short application for each club

Complete the club‑specific application form shared on the club’s profile or social channels. Focus on your motivation, availability, and any prior exposure instead of trying to present a perfect resume.

10–15 questions Deadlines vary by club

Step 03

Attend the welcome call or interview

Shortlisted students receive an invitation to a welcome conversation, which might range from a casual group call to a structured interview. This is your opportunity to ask questions and understand expectations.

Online or on‑campus Meet core team members

Step 04

Join the onboarding sprint and first project

Once confirmed, you will be onboarded into the club’s workspace and communication channels. Expect a short sprint of orientation tasks to learn the tools, workflows, and expectations before diving into a live project or event.

Tools & channels setup First contribution in 2–4 weeks

Set realistic expectations from day one.

Clubs are designed to complement your academics, not compete with them.

  • Most clubs expect a 3–5 hour weekly commitment during active seasons, with lighter workload during exams.
  • Leadership roles typically open after at least one semester of consistent contribution.
  • You may join multiple clubs, but prioritize depth and reliability over quantity.
  • Always respond to club communication promptly, even if it is to decline or reschedule.
